## 2. What Google search can deliver

| | Worst | Moderate | Best |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of searches our ad shows on | 50% | 70% | 85% |
| Ad click rate | 4% | 6% | 8% |
| **Clicks a month** (plus competitor-name clicks) | ~165 | **~310** | ~480 |
| Average cost per click | KSh 84 | KSh 58 | KSh 49 |
| **Monthly cost** | KSh 13,800 | **KSh 17,900** | KSh 23,500 |
| Visitors who start a free trial | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| **Trials a month from Google** | 8 | **31** | 72 |

**Finding:** KSh 15–20k a month is about what Google search in Kenya can absorb for these needs.
- Above about KSh 25–30k a month, extra money buys few extra clicks.
- So the growth beyond Google search has to come from other channels.

## 3. Competitors

- **Uzapoint** (Kenyan):
  - "Trusted by 10,000+ businesses in Africa", with a free trial;
  - wins customers through **SME training workshops**, partners and financing, not mainly ads;
  - builds in **KRA eTIMS** (electronic tax invoices) on every sale;
  - brand searches have climbed steadily since 2024 (Trends), yet its site is below Similarweb's threshold (under about 5,000 visits a month).
- **Loyverse:** a free global POS; 390 searches a month in Kenya.
- **Gap to close:** BizNez has no direct KRA eTIMS link (only an ETR photo or PDF). For businesses that must issue eTIMS invoices this is a deciding feature.

## 4. Market size

Kenya has about **7.4 million MSMEs**: **1.6 million licensed** and 5.8 million unlicensed (KNBS MSME survey 2016; the 2024 survey is under way).
The market is huge; the limit is **reach**, not the number of businesses.

What each assumes:

**Worst:**
- Google search only, a weak landing page and no follow-up;
- 10% of trials pay;
- 8% of paying businesses leave each month.

**Moderate:**
- Google search well run (31 trials a month);
- the website's business-type and town pages ranking on Google, bringing up to 25 free trials a month by month 8;
- the owner selling face to face, about 4 businesses a month;
- a few referrals;
- 20% of trials pay, and 5% leave each month.

**Best**, the target, and what it takes:
- Google search at its best (72 trials a month).
- The website's pages on Google's first page for those 6,500 searches, bringing up to 60 free trials a month by month 8.
- **2 field agents** selling face to face: 10 rising to 24 businesses a month (in Kenya, trust is won in person).
- **3 malls signed in the year**, each bringing about 20 tenants over 3 months.
- Referrals: about 3% of paying businesses bring one more each month (the free-month offer).
- **30% of trials pay** (setup help, WhatsApp follow-up within a day, first sale done in the first session).
- Only **3% leave each month** (quick support, reminders, real value from day one).
- Add **KRA eTIMS** before competing hard in shops.

## 6. What to measure from day one

- Cost per click and per trial, per ad group.
- Trial-to-paid share per business type and per channel.
- Monthly leavers, with reasons.
- Trials from the website's own Google ranking (Search Console).
- Agents: visits, trials and paid per week.
- Referral and mall-tenant signups.

After 8–12 weeks, put money and time into whichever channel turns trials into payments at the lowest cost.
