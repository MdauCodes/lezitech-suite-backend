The short answer

Plan around moderate. It needs nothing special, only the onboarding and WhatsApp selling already built, and ads at KSh 20–40k a month. Worst is what happens if paid ads are the only way you sell: stop ads after six weeks and switch to direct selling. Best is possible but needs fast hiring. The first six weeks of real numbers (cost per trial, trial to paying, who pays a second month) should replace every guess in this document.

What the research says (12 November, still true)

  • Market: about 7.4 million small businesses in Kenya, about 1.56 million licensed (KNBS). BizNez's buyers are licensed businesses with a counter, staff and stock: room for thousands, not millions.
  • KRA eTIMS is the strongest reason to buy now: since January 2024 expenses without an eTIMS invoice can't be claimed, and KRA began cross-checking against eTIMS data from January 2026. Basic compliance is free (eTIMS Lite), so "helps me with KRA" sells, but businesses searching for an eTIMS-integrated POS will pick competitors until BizNez is integrated.
  • Price: Kenyan POS subscriptions mostly KSh 1,000–8,000 a month; Loyverse is free. Lite at KSh 2,650 sits mid-market.
  • Ad costs: no verified Kenyan software figure; a local specialist quotes KSh 20–195 a click. Meta clicks about KSh 10–50; click-to-WhatsApp ads arrived in Kenya in April 2026.
  • Conversion (global benchmarks): visitor to trial 3–8%; trial to paying 8–18.5% (best 25–45%); small-business churn 3–7% a month, likely higher in Kenya.

What decides which one you land in

  1. Measure from week 1: cost per WhatsApp chat, chat to trial, trial to paying, and who pays a second month. After six weeks, put the real numbers into this model.
  2. WhatsApp first, replied to by a person, fast. Google is for the smaller group already searching.
  3. Turn trials into payers: the first-day checklist, the countdown with one M-Pesa payment, and a call in the first week.
  4. Show Growth during the free days: suppliers, moving stock, back-office reports. The plan mix alone is worth about KSh 760k in the year.
  5. Prevent early cancellations: call every new paying business in its first two weeks; offer yearly payment (10 months' price for 12).
  6. eTIMS: start the KRA integrator application now; "KRA-ready" is the strongest message in this market.
  7. If worst shows up by week 6 (fewer than 15 trials a month): cut ads and sell directly: WhatsApp groups, trade and market associations, visiting shops and restaurants, agents paid per paying signup.

How the numbers are worked out, and sources

Each month: trials = (Google budget ÷ cost per click × visitor-to-trial + WhatsApp budget ÷ cost per chat × chat-to-trial) × the season. New paying = last month's trials × trial-to-paying + new paying without ads. Paying = last month's paying × (1 − leaving) + new paying. Money in = paying × revenue per business. The year's result = money in − ads − running costs (KSh 6k a month + 1% M-Pesa collection) − support staff (KSh 45k a month for each 120 paying businesses beyond the first 120) − tax (1.5% Turnover Tax; 16/116 once 12-month turnover passes KSh 5M). It doesn't include your own pay, income tax, hardware or office costs. Worst, moderate and best each use one set of assumptions all year; real life will mix them.

Where this is inference, not data: the season, Kenyan churn above global benchmarks, the plan mix, KSh 45k for a support person and 120 businesses per person, and KSh 6k a month for the server and email. Kenyan software ad costs are not published; the ranges come from one local specialist and global averages.
