# BizNez audit by target customer (2026-11-17)

Scope:
- Everything on `launch` in both repos as of 17 Nov, including the other session's work from 12–16 Nov:
  - restaurants R1–R8, malls and property M1–M9;
  - stock per business kind, packs, sizes and colours, product codes, customer statements;
  - plans and tools, branches, job titles, PayHero, plain-words passes.

How it was checked:
- the sector catalogue (`sectors.json`, `subsectors.json`): every type's own words, switches, screens, seed, questions and KPIs;
- endpoints and screens per sector;
- searches for each checklist item in the API and web code;
- tests and typecheck.

**Not clicked through in the browser** for this audit; scores rest on code and tests.

**Health:**
- API: **211 tests, all passing** (218 after the 17 Nov quick fixes).
- App and website: typecheck clean.
- Test classes: 75. There were **none for hotel rooms, salon bookings, tours and trips, or wholesale quotes** (added 17 Nov).

Legend: ✅ done · 🟡 partly · ❌ missing

## 1. Restaurants & cafés (10 types: restaurant, café, bar, fast food, bakery, rooms, ice cream, grill, canteen, cloud kitchen)
- ✅ Tables, floor, kitchen screen by station, courses, split bills, the right bill (R1).
- ✅ QR ordering, live tracking, pay from the phone (single and split), tips, rating, waiter assignment, substitutes.
- ✅ Group orders: `GroupOrdersTest` and the `GroupTrack` page.
- ✅ Recipes and food cost (R2), waste log and bar tots (R3), table bookings (R4).
- ✅ Delivery with areas, riders and cash hand-in (R5); combos, happy hour and meal times (R6).
- ✅ Bakery day and cake orders, grill by the kg (R7); night summary (R8); waiter end of shift; loyalty; board screens.
- 🟡 Delivery-app orders (Glovo, Uber Eats, Bolt) are entered by hand with the app's cut. There is no integration (acceptable for launch).
- 🟡 "restaurant" itself has no seed or switches of its own and relies on the sector defaults (fine).

**Score: 9/10.** The most complete segment.

## 2. Hotels, lodges & guest houses (6 types: hotel, safari lodge, guest house, serviced apartments, Airbnb host, conference)
- ✅ Front desk, room chart, check-in and check-out, folio (charge to room), deposits, housekeeping states, restaurant and bar.
- ✅ ~~No calendar sync with Airbnb or Booking.com.~~ iCal both ways per room, read every 15 minutes (phase 3).
- ✅ ~~No online room booking.~~ `/stay/{slug}` with prices for the stay, optional M-Pesa deposit, guest page and cancel (phase 3).
- ✅ ~~No guest register.~~ ID or passport, nationality, coming from and car at check-in, required if switched on, printable (phase 3).
- ✅ ~~No seasonal or weekend rates.~~ Prices by dates and weekdays, per room, type or all, with shortest stay (phase 3).
- 🟡 Halls: bookings carry the event, organiser and people; no separate day view yet.
- ✅ Tests added 17 Nov: `RoomsAndStaysTest` (no double booking, check-in, charge to room, check-out to folio, housekeeping, no-show, privacy).
- 🟡 4 of the 6 types (hotel, guest house, Airbnb host, conference) have no seed, words or switches of their own (phase 9).

**Score: 5/10 → 8/10 after phase 3.**

## 3. Shops & minimarts (11 types: minimart, boutique, phones, hardware, agrovet, bookshop, liquor, butchery, auto spares, petrol station, gas & water)
- ✅ Barcodes (on when used), forgiving search, product codes.
- ✅ Packs (carton/piece), sizes and colours.
- ✅ Opening stock, stock-take on phones, use-by and expiry.
- ✅ Credit, who owes you, statements; lowest price and price control.
- ✅ Transfers from store to shops, weigh-at-till.
- ❌ **Phone shops: no IMEI or serial numbers, no warranty tracking.**
- ❌ **Petrol stations: no pumps, nozzles, meter readings or litres reconciliation.** Fuel is sold like shop items.
- ❌ **Gas & water: no cylinder exchange or deposit (empties).**
- ❌ Liquor: no age-check prompt or licence hours.
- 🟡 Hardware and auto spares: one wholesale price only, no price tiers (retail, contractor, trade).

**Score: 7/10.** Strong for general shops; weak for 3 of the 11 types.

## 4. Salons, barbers & beauty (8 open types + clinic coming soon)
- ✅ Bookings by staff, no-shows, checkout, commissions, services that use up products, client notes, loyalty.
- ✅ Appointment reminders by email, 24 h and 2 h before, built 17 Nov (phase 2). SMS and WhatsApp come with Africa's Talking (phase 10).
- ✅ Online booking page for clients at `/book/{slug}`, built 17 Nov (phase 2).
- ✅ Deposit when booking (M-Pesa, 17 Nov). ❌ No packages or memberships yet (phase 8).
- ❌ **Gym: no memberships, renewals or expiry**, though the gym type calls customers "Members".
- 🟡 Car wash: no queue or vehicle number on the wash.
- ✅ Tests added 17 Nov: `AppointmentsTest` (no double booking a stylist, checkout credited to them, no-show, privacy).
- 🟡 Hair salon (the main type) has no seed, words or switches of its own.

**Score: 5/10.**

## 5. Wholesale & distribution (5 types: packaging, FMCG, distributor, manufacturer, building materials)
- ✅ Quotes to invoices, wholesale price with minimum quantity, credit limits.
- ✅ Statements and ageing, packs, supplier orders, store-to-shop transfers.
- ❌ No price tiers per customer group or volume breaks beyond one wholesale price.
- ❌ **No delivery notes or dispatch / proof of delivery.**
- ❌ No sales-rep routes or van sales (the distributor type promises reps).
- ❌ Manufacturer: no bill of materials or production runs (restaurant recipes aren't offered here).
- ✅ Tests added 17 Nov: quotes can't be paid until converted (`TripsAndQuotesTest`).
- 🟡 **All 5 types have no seed, words or switches of their own.** Every wholesaler gets identical setup.

**Score: 5/10.**

## 6. Pharmacies & chemists (coming soon, 4 types)
- 🟡 Prescription-needed flag and expiry exist.
- ❌ Batch or lot numbers per delivery, a controlled-drugs register and dispensing records are missing.
- ❌ None of the 4 types has its own pack content.
- Hidden from sign-up as decided, which is right.

**Score: 3/10** (not ready to open, as planned).

## 7. Service businesses & agencies (12 types)
- ✅ Leads pipeline with stages and follow-ups, quotes, invoices, deposits, statements, credit.
- ❌ **Garage: "Job card" is only a word.** No vehicle number, no job-card steps (received, diagnosed, waiting parts, ready), no mechanic per job.
- ❌ **Laundry: no "ready for collection" status, tags or collection.**
- ❌ **School: no classes, terms, fee structures or fee statements per student** (it's called "Fee invoice" but works like a sale).
- ❌ Tailoring: no measurements or fitting dates.
- ❌ No recurring invoices (retainers, monthly cleaning contracts) and no timesheets or projects for agencies and consultancies.
- 🟡 5 types (agency, consultancy, logistics, cleaning, repairs) have no seed, words or switches of their own.

**Score: 4/10.** The words promise more than the screens do.

## 8. Safaris & tour operators (5 types)
- ✅ Departures with seats, vehicle and guide; bookings with deposits and balances; manifest.
- ❌ No itineraries (day by day), no supplier costs per trip (park fees, lodges) and no profit per trip.
- ❌ **Car hire is set up as "trips".** There is no fleet, rental agreement, mileage or return check.
- ~~Bug: the tours home screen's `departures_next_30` figure is missing.~~ **Wrong, corrected 17 Nov:** it exists in both the API and the home screen; the audit's search missed names with digits.
- ✅ Tests added 17 Nov: `TripsAndQuotesTest` (no overselling seats, deposit on the booking, other businesses can't see it).
- 🟡 3 types (safari operator, tour company, travel agency) have nothing of their own.

**Score: 4/10.**

## 9. Malls, landlords & property (5 types: mall, market stalls, commercial building, apartments, property agent)
- ✅ Leases and rent: M-Pesa, paybill matching, penalties, statements, increases, deposits, renewals, move-out.
- ✅ Tenant portal, repairs, notices; meters and service charge.
- ✅ Market collectors, landlords and agent commission, rental tax (MRI) and WHT.
- ✅ Floor maps, to-let page, mall directory, food court.
- 🟡 4 types have no pack content of their own, but the features cover them.

**Score: 9/10.**
