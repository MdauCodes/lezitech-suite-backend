BizNez ushers

Two friendly guides who appear across BizNez the way Trendysh's usher does: full body, a clear facial expression, a gesture that points the user where to look or what to do next, and sometimes a prop (a phone, a checklist, a receipt). Same flat, clean illustration style as the Trendysh usher, but dressed smart-casual and jua kali for Kenyan SME owners and their staff: no suits. Generate them one image at a time.

What we learned from the Trendysh usher

  • One person, always the same (same face, hair and outfit), so customers start to recognise her as "the one who helps".
  • Full body, standing, on a clear background, about 3:5 tall. On screen she is small: 130 to 190 pixels high.
  • Her gesture does the talking: an open palm presenting what's beside her (welcome), a wave with a ticked checklist ("received, all done"), a phone held up saying "Message us", a hand toward a numbered list (how it works).
  • She always sits with a short heading, one sentence and one clear button: "No orders yet / Start shopping", "Order received", "Payment received", "A question? / WhatsApp us". She never replaces the words; she makes them friendlier.
  • Her clothes carry the brand's accent colour (Trendysh: a magenta belt and shoes). BizNez: lime green (#C6F432) touches.
  • She's decoration for the eyes, not for screen readers (no alt text), so the heading and button must make sense on their own.

1. How to get the same person every time

  1. Make the character sheet first (section 3) and choose the one you like. That image is the reference.
  2. For every pose after that, attach the chosen character sheet and paste: the master style, then the character's description, then one pose. Ask: "Same character as the attached image, same face, hair, clothes and colours."
  3. One pose per image. Ask for a transparent background (PNG); if the tool can't, a plain white background is fine and LeziTech will cut it out.
  4. Keep the whole body in the frame with a little space around it; no shadows on the floor, no scenery.
  5. Save each with the file name shown. Redo any where the hands, the pointing direction or the face are off.

2. Master style (paste first, every time)

Flat vector illustration of a friendly Kenyan guide character for a business app, full body, standing, clean smooth shapes, soft subtle shading, crisp outlines, warm natural skin tones, expressive face with clear emotion, natural hands with five fingers, modern app-illustration style (like a friendly onboarding guide), transparent background, no floor, no scenery, the character centred with space around, portrait 3:5, high resolution 1200x2000.

4. Avoid list (negative prompt)

suit, tie, blazer, high heels, corporate office, photo-realistic, 3D render, anime, chibi, exaggerated cartoon proportions, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed hands, text in the image (except the few words named in a pose), logos, brand names, watermark, background scenery, floor shadow, cropped feet, cropped head, different face from the reference, different clothes from the reference.

5. Poses

"Points right" means toward the right edge of the picture: the content sits on that side in the app. Each pose says where it's used.

6. Where to put the finished images

Save each PNG with its exact file name into lezitech-suite-web/public/ushers/ (or send them to LeziTech). BizNez shows each one by itself on the screens below as soon as the file is there; until then those screens simply show no picture.
