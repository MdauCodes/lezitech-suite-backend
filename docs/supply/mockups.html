The market buyer's phone

  • Float that disappears. Money in hand goes down with each purchase, so the evening cash-up knows what he should bring back.

The client's receiving clerk

The hotel or hospital clerk gets an email with a link when the vehicle arrives. They count, weigh, fill in what they received, sign and confirm.

What this stops

  • Short deliveries that nobody notices until the invoice. The gap shows the moment the clerk confirms.
  • Paying for goods that never arrived. The invoice follows what the clerk confirmed, which is why hotels like it too.
  • "The hotel rejected it" stories. Rejects and their reasons are recorded by the client, and the goods must come back to the store.
