# Lezi Suite: what a client needs, and what they get

For the LeziTech team when talking to a business. The public version is the website page `/what-you-need`.
Only list what Lezi Suite does today.

## Works on

Phones (Android, iPhone), tablets, laptops and desktops, in the browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari).
Nothing to install. One device is enough to start; add hardware later.

## Selling (POS)

**Needs**
- A phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser.
- Internet (mobile data is fine). The till keeps selling if it drops and sends the sales when it's back.
- Their own M-Pesa till or paybill, for payment prompts (or they type the M-Pesa code by hand).

**Helps, not required**
- Receipt printer, 58mm or 80mm, USB or Bluetooth (80mm is the default setting). On Android, Bluetooth printers
  usually print through an app such as RawBT or the printer's own print service.
- Barcode scanner, USB or Bluetooth (it types like a keyboard).
- Cash drawer that opens from the receipt printer (set in the printer's own settings).
- A tablet for the kitchen screen, and any TV with a browser for the "order ready" board.
- A card machine from their bank (card sales are recorded as card).

**Does**
- Cash, M-Pesa prompts, card and pay-later, split payments, deposits.
- Receipts printed (58mm, 80mm or A4 invoice), automatic printing after each sale, on WhatsApp or by email.
- Tables, kitchen screen and kitchen tickets, split bills, bookings, rooms.
- Staff PINs and switching cashier, opening and closing the till, refunds, manager approval for discounts.

## Customers & sales leads (CRM)

**Needs**: a phone or computer; WhatsApp on their phone; an email address.
**Helps**: a WhatsApp Business number (receipts then come from it); their customer list in Excel.
**Does**: customers with balances, credit limits and loyalty points; sales leads with follow-up reminders;
price quotes that become sales; reports on best customers, repeat customers and leads won or lost.

## Back office (ERP)

**Needs**: a laptop or desktop is easiest (a phone works); their KRA PIN; their item list (typed or Excel).
**Helps**: supplier names and numbers; a printer for stock counts.
**Does**: stock per shop and store with transfers; buying from suppliers; batches and expiry; stock counts and
write-offs; reports on stock value, running low, who owes them and who they owe, profit by category;
a VAT and turnover tax summary.

**Not yet**: expenses and full profit and loss, KRA eTIMS invoices (switched off until KRA approval).

## Good to have

A small data bundle, power backup (power bank or UPS) for the till device, and one device kept at the counter.

## Something else built

Websites, custom systems, mobile apps, connections to other systems, branding: the website page `/custom`, or
Settings, "Need something built?" inside the app. Both land in the control panel under Requests, marked
"custom work".
